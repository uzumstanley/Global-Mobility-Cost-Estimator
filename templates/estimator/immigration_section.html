Immigration & Work Permits

  • Recommended visa route: Global Business Mobility (Senior or Specialist Worker) – no English test, fits 3‑year prior service and salary threshold.
  • Alternative route: Skilled Worker – English test required.
  • Business visit / ETA: Swiss nationals need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (£20) for pre‑assignment visits; cannot work on the ETA.
  • Passport validity: Must be valid for the entire assignment duration (no 6‑month rule).
  • Employer sponsor duties: Hold a GBM licence, issue a Certificate of Sponsorship, pay Immigration Skills Charge, and run PAYE/RTI with NIC exempt under Certificate of Coverage.
